About Bombardier

"Bombardier Inc. is a Canadian aerospace manufacturer headquartered in Montreal...with major businesses in rail transportation, recreational products, and aircraft manufacturing. After selling off those businesses, Bombardier has focused on the design, manufacture, and servicing of business jets, including the Challenger and Global lines of aircraft." (Encyclopaedia Britannica, n.d.)

The founding of Bombardier

Bombardier was founded by joseph-Armad Bombardier, a Canadian inventor from Quebec, in 1937 he started making the invention known as the snow mobile and in 1942 Bombardier was officially founded in order to produce the B2 snowmobile Back then called L’Auto-Neige Bombardier Limitée which manufactured and industrialized the snow mobile after world war II. (Encyclopaedia Britannica, n.d.) However Bombardier would not enter the aviation industry until the 1980s after bombardier became a leader in rail transportation.

Entry into the Aviation Industry

"Sixteen years after diversifying into rail transportation, Bombardier took flight in the aerospace sector in 1986 with the purchases of Canadair, the leading Canadian aircraft manufacturer of Challenger widebody business jets,"(bombardier,n.d.)
